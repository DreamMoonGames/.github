## About

A small independent studio that cares about atmosphere over spectacle — quiet moments, monochrome
paper-cut silhouettes, moonlight, and stories told with restraint. Everything is built on
**Unreal Engine 5**, from the renderer to the interface.

Most of what we make is still behind closed doors. This page is the front door.

## What we build

- **Games** — original single-player titles with a storybook, black-and-white art direction.
- **Hertz Toolchain** — 20+ open-source Unreal plugins that turn materials, effects and textures
  into source code: DreamShader · DreamFX · DreamTexture · DreamGUI.
- **Tools** — internal pipelines and editor tooling that keep a tiny team fast.
